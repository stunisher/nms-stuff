# NMS Jetpack Tank Bonus Recipes (320%+)

All recipes yield 320%+ Jetpack Tank bonuses for 10 minutes when processed in the Nutrient Processor.
Data sourced from AssistantNMS WebApp (app.nmsassistant.com) and in-game verification.

---

## Common Sub-Component: Cake Batter

Most high-tier jetpack recipes require **Cake Batter** as a base. Full raw breakdown:

| Step | Process |
| :---- | :---- |
| Heptaploid Wheat → | **Refined Flour** |
| Any Milk → Cream → | **Churned Butter** |
| Churned Butter + Processed Sugar → | **Sweetened Butter** *(or Honey Butter: Churned Butter + Synthetic Honey)* |
| **Sweetened/Honey Butter + Refined Flour + Any Egg** → | **Cake Batter** |

**Raw materials needed for Cake Batter:** Heptaploid Wheat, Any Milk, Any Egg, Grahberry/Sweetroot/Cactus Nectar (for Processed Sugar) *or* Sticky Honey (for Synthetic Honey)

## Notes

- **Horrifying, Gooey Delight** also appears in the Fauna scanning bonus list (+326% Fauna) — it dual-purposes as both a jetpack and fauna scanning dish depending on context. The Nutrient Processor bonus applied depends on game state/active buffs.
- **Proto-Batter** and **Wailing Batter** can substitute for Cake Batter in the Horrifying, Gooey Delight recipe.
- **Stellar Custard** (Silicon Egg + Cream + Processed Sugar) is the key sub-component for the top-tier Interstellar Fancy. Silicon Egg is made from Chromatic Metal in the Nutrient Processor.

### Key corrections from the original document:

1. **Wriggling Doughnut** — The original listed `Larval Core + Processed Sugar → Wriggling Jam`, but the wiki says it's **`Crab 'Apple' + Processed Sugar`**. Also corrected Lumpen Doughnut to use **Clarified Oil** (same fix as the Fauna section).

2. **Appalling Jam Sponge** — The original listed **Extra-Fluffy Batter** as the batter, but the wiki clearly states it uses **Wailing Batter** (made with Larval Core, not eggs).

3. **Primordial Sponge** — The original listed `Extra-Fluffy Batter + Awful Mush`. The wiki says it's actually **`Wailing Batter + Sweetened Mucous + Cream`**. Both the batter and the filling are completely different. Sweetened Mucous comes from `Nourishing Slime + Processed Sugar`.

4. **Cake of the Lost** — The original listed **Monstrous Custard** as the primary pairing. The wiki lists **Viscous Custard** as the primary, with Monstrous and others as alternates. Monstrous Custard also requires three ingredients (`Larval Core + Cream + Processed Sugar`), not two.

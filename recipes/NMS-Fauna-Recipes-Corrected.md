### Key corrections from the original document:

1. **Anomalous Doughnut** — The Lumpen Doughnut recipe was wrong. The original listed `Dough + Churned Butter + Processed Sugar`, but the wiki says it's **`Clarified Oil + Dough + Processed Sugar`**. Clarified Oil itself requires two butters (e.g. Churned Butter × 2).

2. **Nectar Sponge Cake** — The original claimed `Cactus Nectar + Processed Sugar → Syrupy Nectar`, but **Syrupy Nectar is not a crafted item** — it's an **animal byproduct harvested from Floradae** creatures.

3. **Horrifying, Gooey Delight** — The original's Monstrous Custard recipe was `Larval Core + Cream`, but the wiki lists **three ingredients: `Larval Core + Cream + Processed Sugar`**.

4. **All links** — Updated from `nomanssky.fandom.com` to `nomanssky.miraheze.org`.

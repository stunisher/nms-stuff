### Key corrections from the original document:

1. **Cake of Sin** — The original described the recipe as `Writhing, Roiling Batter + Ever-burning Jam` using Fireberry. The wiki says the primary recipe is **`Writhing, Roiling Batter + Crab 'Apple'`**, with Wriggling Jam + Cream as an alternative. Ever-burning Jam is not used in this dish.

2. **Caramel Curiosity** — Confirmed correct in structure. Added the shortcut: **`Root Juice → Crunchy Caramel`** directly as an alternative.

3. **Wailing Caramel Cake** — The original listed `Refined Flour + Vile Pod + Delicate Meringue → Wailing Batter`. This is incorrect — the wiki says **Wailing Batter uses Larval Core**, not Vile Pod. The correct recipe is `Any Butter + Refined Flour + Larval Core`.

4. **Salt-Laced Honey Cake** — The original listed `Refined Flour + Any Egg + Churned Butter → Thick, Sweet Batter`, but the wiki says it requires **Gooey Butter or Gooey ProtoButter** (not plain Churned Butter). Gooey Butter is made from `Churned Butter + Sticky 'Honey'`. The Salty Custard recipe was also wrong — the original said `Custard + Salt` but the wiki shows **`Salt + Cream + Processed Sugar`** (three ingredients).
